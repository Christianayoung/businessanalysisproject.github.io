Restaurant Order and Inventory Management Improvement.

This client-restaurant got in contact with us because they were experiencing frequent order delays, stock shortages and food wastage. After analysis, we discovered that customer orders and inventory were being managed manually, making it difficult to track stock levels and prepare orders efficiently. We also identified inconsistent stock records, delays in updating inventory, difficulty monitoring fast-moving items, and a lack of real-time information for staff when making purchasing and ordering decisions.
